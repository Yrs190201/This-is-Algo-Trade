SMA Crossover Trading Strategy

Classic dual moving-average crossover system with simulated price data

How it works:
  • Buy when the short SMA crosses above the long SMA (bullish crossover)
  • Sell when the short SMA crosses below the long SMA (bearish crossover)
  • This is a trend-following strategy. It works best in trending markets and can produce false signals in sideways markets.

Blue = Price • Cyan = Short SMA • Orange = Long SMA • Green ▲ = Buy • Red ▼ = Sell
